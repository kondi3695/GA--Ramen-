The Beginning

One Bowl Changed Everything

It was 2011. Our founder, Kenji Matsuda, sat in a tiny 8-seat ramen-ya in Fukuoka at 2am after missing his flight home. The bowl of tonkotsu placed in front of him — thick, cloudy, impossibly rich — changed his life forever.

He spent three years apprenticing under Master Hiroshi Yamamoto, learning to make broth from bones, noodles from flour, and perfection from patience. In 2014, Tanuki Ramen was born — a small pop-up that became a restaurant, and then a movement.

Today we craft hundreds of bowls daily, but every single one carries that same soul from that 2am discovery in Fukuoka.

— Kenji Matsuda, Founder

Our Journey

How We Got Here

From a late-night bowl in Fukuoka to thousands of happy customers.

2011
The Revelation
Kenji discovers the world's best tonkotsu in a hole-in-the-wall in Fukuoka. Begins a three-year apprenticeship.
2014
First Pop-Up
Tanuki Ramen launches as a weekend pop-up in the Eastside Market. Lines stretch around the block every Saturday.
2016
Brick & Mortar
We open our first restaurant, seating 42. Fully booked for the next 8 months. Michelin Bib Gourmand awarded.
2019
Online Delivery
We launch delivery with insulated ramen kits — bringing our 18-hour broth to doorsteps across the city.
2025
New Chapter
Expanded menu, nationwide shipping kits & a new Tanuki ramen school for home cooks who want to learn the craft.
