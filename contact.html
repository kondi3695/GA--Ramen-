Frequently Asked

Quick Answers

How long does delivery take?
Most deliveries arrive in 30–40 minutes. During peak hours you'll receive live tracking via SMS once your order is picked up.
Do you offer catering for events?
Yes! We cater private dinners, corporate lunches and large events. Minimum 10 people. Contact us via the form above for a custom quote.
Are there vegetarian or vegan options?
Absolutely. Our Veggie Shio Ramen, Zaru Soba and Edamame are all vegetarian. Most dishes can be modified — just leave a note at checkout.
How do I apply a discount code?
In your cart you'll see a "Promo Code" field. Enter your code and hit Apply. Visit the Sales page for all current offers including TANUKI20.
What if my order arrives incorrect?
Email hello@tanukiramen.com or call us within 30 minutes of delivery and we'll resend or fully refund immediately — no questions asked.
Is there a loyalty programme?
Yes! Earn 10 points per $1 spent. Reach 500 points for Gold status with perks. Redeem for free bowls, sides and mystery rewards.
