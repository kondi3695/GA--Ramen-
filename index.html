FAQ

Frequently Asked

How long does delivery take?
Most deliveries arrive within 30–40 minutes. During peak hours (Friday evenings, weekends), expect up to 50 minutes. You'll receive live tracking via SMS once your order is picked up.
Do you offer catering for events?
Yes! We cater for private dinners, corporate lunches, birthdays and large events. Minimum 10 people. Contact us via the form above or email catering@tanukiramen.com for a custom quote.
Are there vegetarian or vegan options?
Absolutely. Our Veggie Shio Ramen, Zaru Soba and Edamame are all vegetarian. We can also modify most dishes — just leave a note at checkout. Vegan options are clearly marked on our menu.
Can I customise my bowl?
Of course! Use the special instructions field at checkout to customise spice level, noodle firmness, toppings, or anything else. Our chefs will do their best to accommodate every preference.
How do I apply a discount code?
In your cart, you'll see a "Promo Code" field. Type your code there and hit Apply. Valid codes include TANUKI20, TANUKI50, LUNCH30 and more — check our Sales page for current offers!
What if my order arrives cold or incorrect?
We're so sorry if that happens — it's not okay and we'll make it right. Email hello@tanukiramen.com or call us directly within 30 minutes of delivery and we'll resend or refund immediately.
Do you ship nationwide?
We currently deliver hot bowls in Manhattan, Brooklyn & Queens. However, we do ship DIY Ramen Kits nationwide — full 18-hour broth, fresh noodles and toppings — via our online shop. Coming soon!
Is there a loyalty programme?
Yes! Earn 10 points per $1 spent. Reach 500 points for Gold status with exclusive perks. Redeem points for free bowls, sides and mystery rewards. Sign up free via your account page.
